# Distinct cellular and molecular endometrial characteristics are associated with different causes of abnormal uterine bleeding

Abstract: Abnormal uterine bleeding (AUB) affects up to 50% of reproductive-aged women and girls, negatively impacting quality of life, representing a global health burden. The symptom of AUB, has multiple causes, yet endometrial mechanisms underlying AUB remain largely unexplored, limiting effective medical management. We investigated meticulously categorised and menstrual cycle-staged endometrium from women with AUB and uterine fibroids (AUB-L) and primary endometrial disorder (AUB-E) finding, the endometrial transcriptome was determined by the underlying cause of AUB. Different causes of AUB revealed altered spatial immunolocalisation of sex steroid receptors. Endometrial expression of candidate progesterone dependent genes was not altered. Deep interrogation confirmed menstrual cycle stage as a major determinant influencing cycling endometrium of women with AUB. AUB should thus not be conceptualised as a single endometrial phenotype because the clinical symptom is shared. Future treatment development should target different mechanistic pathways, dependent on underlying cause of AUB, to improve clinical care outcomes.

This repository contains code used for the RNA-seq analysis of AUB-L vs AUB-E.
